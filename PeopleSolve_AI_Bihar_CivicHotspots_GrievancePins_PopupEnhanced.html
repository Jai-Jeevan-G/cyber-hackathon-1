Geographic reference

Neighbouring areas: Nepal to the north; Uttar Pradesh to the west; Jharkhand to the south; West Bengal to the east.

River systems: the Ganga crosses Bihar broadly west-to-east. Important tributary systems include the Gandak, Burhi Gandak, Bagmati, Kosi, Kamla-Balan, Mahananda, Son, Punpun and Falgu. Flood exposure varies by basin and season; map labels alone do not establish current flood risk.

Terrain: much of north Bihar is a low alluvial plain with many Himalayan-fed rivers; southern Bihar transitions toward older uplands and plateau margins near Jharkhand. Roads and railways connect Patna with district headquarters and regional towns.

Map layers: use the base map for roads, towns and river labels; select a division or district below to centre the map. Civic complaint pins remain demo positions until each complaint location is geocoded.

Reference basis: Government of Bihar district/administrative listings and Survey of India’s 2025 Bihar state map. Figures are reference information, not live statistics. Verify boundaries, district changes and field coordinates against official GIS before operational decisions.
